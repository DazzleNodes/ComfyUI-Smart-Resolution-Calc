## Caveats

A kill proves the suite noticed *this* change, not that it tests the right thing. A clean sweep is evidence about the mutations tried, not proof of suite completeness. The live tests call `app.handleFile` directly; the DOM drop and File > Open paths that reach it are covered by the checklist's manual steps.
