## Anomalies

Round 1 printed two `UnicodeDecodeError: 'charmap' codec can't decode byte 0x9d` tracebacks from the harness's subprocess reader threads: the harness decodes captured output with the locale codec (cp1252) while vitest prints UTF-8 check marks. Verdicts come from exit codes, not captured text, and every survivor was re-verified in isolation, so round 1's verdicts stand; rounds 1b and 2 ran under `PYTHONUTF8=1` with no anomaly. Reported to the skill owner as a harness bug (decode with `errors='replace'` or UTF-8).

Restore: `restore_ok` true for all 22 mutants; `git status --short` identical to the pre-sweep capture except for the `tests/mutation/` directory created by the sweep itself; source hash unchanged; full suite 77/77 after the last round.

## Caveats

A kill proves the suite noticed *this* change, not that it tests the right thing. A clean sweep is evidence about the mutations tried, not proof of suite completeness.
